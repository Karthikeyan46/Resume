Background & Vision

About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Jain University, Bangalore. Having submitted my doctoral dissertation at Galgotias University, my academic journey centers around the intersection of bio-inspired cryptographic protocols, secure edge networking, and machine intelligence.

Over the past decade, I have served across premier engineering institutions in South and North India, including Galgotias University (Greater Noida), Knowledge Institute of Technology (KIOT, Salem), and SNS College of Technology (Coimbatore). Throughout this tenure, I have held pivotal academic administrative responsibilities as IQAC Coordinator, Project Coordinator, Moodle Administrator, and Training & Placement Lead.

My core research investigates DNA Cryptography—harnessing biological nucleotide structures for high-entropy encryption—and developing lightweight security frameworks tailored for constrained Internet of Things (IoT) edge ecosystems.

DNA Cryptography & Cloud Security

Developing bio-inspired encryption architectures using synthetic DNA sequence substitution, X.509 digital attribute certificates, and decoy forensics (DFD) for tamper-proof cloud storage and authentication.

Lightweight IoT & Network Security

Engineering low-overhead cryptographic primitives and LZW compression pipelines optimized for battery- and compute-constrained Internet of Things sensors deployed in Smart Cities and industrial telemetry.
